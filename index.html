What is NutriNext?

NutriNext is a personalized nutrition-tracking app that looks past the calorie count to figure out what your body is actually getting — and what it isn't. It takes your age, height, weight, activity level, and goal, and turns them into daily targets built for you, not a generic average pulled from a general population.

Most nutrition apps stop at logging: you eat, you get a number. NutriNext goes a step further by turning that log into a full loop — you eat, it tracks, it analyzes, it identifies the gaps, it recommends, and your habits improve. Instead of leaving you to interpret raw macros on your own, it tells you plainly what's missing and what to eat next to fix it.

It's also built specifically with Indian eating habits in mind — daal, sabzi, rotis, and regional snacks are already in the food database, not bolted on as an afterthought to a Western food list. Whether the goal is losing weight, gaining weight, or simply maintaining it, NutriNext adjusts its targets and recommendations accordingly.

The problem

Most people can tell you roughly how much they ate today. Far fewer can tell you whether it actually gave their body what it needed. Calorie counts alone don't answer that — and most tracking apps stop right there, leaving you to guess at the rest.

Beyond the numbers

A Daily Nutrition Score rates how well your intake matches your targets — rewarding balance rather than just low calories. Water intake, meal history, and streaks round out the picture, so progress is something you can actually see day to day.
